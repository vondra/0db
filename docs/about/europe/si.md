## Road traffic

DRSI (Direkcija RS za infrastrukturo) publishes only aggregate traffic reports. No per-segment CSV or shapefile found on podatki.gov.si. OSM road class defaults applied.

## Railway

SŽ (Slovenske železnice) does not publish GTFS publicly. The OPSI dataset "Register linijskih odsekov" contains only bus routes. National rail not yet enriched.

## Industrial

- GPPD power plants (NACE 35)

## Buildings

- Overture Maps global baseline only

## Validation

Slovenia implements END via the Law on Environmental Protection (ZVO-1). Strategic noise maps produced by ARSO (Environmental Agency).
