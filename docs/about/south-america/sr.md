## Railway

### Defunct. All railway traffic set to zero.

Bauxite mining railway (Paramaribo–Onverwacht) closed when Alcoa/Suralco smelter closed 2015.

## Industrial

### GEM — 4 plants, operating, ~250 MW

Hydro 1 + thermal/oil 2 + solar 1. **Afobaka hydro 189 MW** (Brokopondo Reservoir, Suriname River) — sole large hydro, provides ~50% of national electricity. Plus Saramacca HFO ~36 MW, a Staatsolie refinery CHP unit, and a small Lelydorp solar farm.

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Bauxite legacy**: Paranam smelter (BHP/Alcoa Suralco) closed 2015; Brokopondo aluminium complex historic
- **Gold mining (interior)**: artisanal (garimpeiro) and licensed operations; Maroon communities, interior rivers
- **Oil/gas (Staatsolie)**: Saramacca offshore discovery 2020 — upcoming offshore development
- **93% forest cover**: Amazon basin, among world's highest — logging and biodiversity corridor
- **Dutch-speaking**: only Dutch-speaking country in South America; multi-ethnic (Hindustani, Creole, Javanese, Maroon)
