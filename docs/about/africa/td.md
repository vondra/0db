## Road traffic

### Class defaults only

Chad Ministry of Infrastructure publishes no open AADT. Fall back to class defaults with N'Djamena Tier-1 boost. Among Africa's least developed road networks.

**Tier-1 metro** (×2.0): **N'Djamena** (~1.5M — capital, Chari+Logone confluence, across from Kousseri/Cameroon).

**Tier-2 cities** (×1.4): **Moundou** (~200k — 2nd city, southern Chad, cotton/Gala brewery), **Sarh** (~130k — 3rd city, former Fort-Archambault), **Abéché** (~120k — eastern Chad, Darfur refugee transit, Wadai sultanate), **Kélo** (~80k — cotton, Mayo-Kebbi), **Doba** (~60k — oil fields, ExxonMobil/Savannah), **Bongor** (~60k — Logone River).

## Railway

### Chad has NEVER had a railway.

One of few large African countries without any rail infrastructure. All freight moves by road via Cameroon (Douala port).

## Industrial

### GEM — 1 plant, operating, 60 MW

**Farcha 60 MW** (diesel, N'Djamena) — the ONLY GEM-listed plant. ~11% electrification, one of world's lowest.

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Doba Basin oil** (ExxonMobil→Savannah Energy) — **Chad-Cameroon pipeline** 1,070 km to Kribi (2003)
- **Cement**: Cimenterie Nationale du Tchad (Pala)
- **Gala Brewery** (Moundou — Castel Group)
- **CotonTchad** (Moundou/Sarh — cotton ginning)
- **Natron** extraction (Lake Chad basin)
- **Lake Chad** — shrunk 90% since 1960s (25,000→~1,350 km²), ecological catastrophe
