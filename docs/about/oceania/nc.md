## Road traffic

### Road defaults

New Caledonia publishes no open per-segment AADT, so roads fall back to the global class defaults scaled by New Caledonia's traffic factor **≈ 0.712** (population density). Only the major classes (motorway, trunk, primary, and their on/off-ramps) are scaled; local roads and the vehicle mix use the global default — the engine applies no per-city tiers or country-specific splits.

| OSM class | Default AADT |
|---|---:|
| Motorway | 30,000 × 0.712 ≈ 21,360 |
| Trunk | 15,000 × 0.712 ≈ 10,680 |
| Primary | 9,000 × 0.712 ≈ 6,408 |
| Secondary / tertiary / residential | 3,000 / 800 / 500 (world default) |
| Service / track / unclassified | 250 / 5 / 1,340 (world default) |

## Railway

### New Caledonia has NO public railway.

No rail infrastructure. All transport by road. Nickel ore moves by truck from mines to smelters.

## Industrial

### GEM — 39 plants, operating, ~970 MW

Solar 24 + wind 7 + coal/unknown 4 + oil/gas 3 + hydro 1 — an unusually dense GEM footprint for a 270k-population territory. Three nickel smelters dominate:

**Doniambo** (SLN/Eramet, Nouméa) — 180+160 = **340 MW** fuel oil. Operating since 1910 — **one of world's oldest continuously operating nickel smelters**. Pyrometallurgical.

**Koniambo Nickel** (Glencore/SMSP, North Province) — 2×135 = **270 MW**. Opened 2013. Pyrometallurgical.

**Goro** (Prony Resources, formerly Vale NC, South Province) — 2×50 = **100 MW**. Hydrometallurgical. Troubled $9B project, multiple ownership changes.

**Yaté 68 MW** hydro. **Jacques Lekawe 55 MW** diesel. ~39 MW wind (Col de Prony cluster, Kafeate, Negandi). ~90 MW solar (20+ projects across North + South Provinces).

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Nickel mining**: NC has **~25% of world's known nickel reserves** — world's #4 producer. Three smelters + numerous open-pit mines across Grande Terre.
- **Chrome/cobalt/manganese** — nickel by-products
- **World's largest lagoon** (UNESCO, 2nd largest coral reef after Great Barrier Reef)
- **2024 political crisis** — riots over electoral reform, Kanak independence movement
