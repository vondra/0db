## Railway

### Ferrocarriles de Cuba — active, main + western + other lines

Cuba operates the **only active national railway in the Caribbean**, ~4,000 km total. Havana–Santiago main line ~861 km. Western suburban lines serve Havana commuters. Freight (sugar, nickel) + long-distance passenger. Chinese rolling stock delivered 2019–22.

| Service type | Typical frequency | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Main line (Havana–Santiago) | 1–2 trains/day | Slow, often delayed |
| Western lines (Havana suburban) | Multiple daily | Commuter service |
| Branch lines | Irregular | Freight-dominant |

## Industrial

### GEM — 38 plants, operating, ~2,891 MW

Thermal (oil/HFO) 14 + diesel 8 + solar 8 + wind 4 + bioenergy 4. **Antonio Guiteras 330 MW** oil — Cuba's largest. **Máximo Gómez 290 MW** + **Mariel 200 MW** oil. Soviet-era fleet with severe reliability issues.

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Nickel/cobalt**: Moa Bay (Sherritt International) — world's 5th largest reserves
- **Sugar**: collapsed from ~8M t/yr (1970) to <1M t/yr; former centrales now minimal
- **Rum/tobacco**: Havana Club, Cohiba — state enterprises, export culture
- **Vintage cars (almendrones)**: pre-1960 US vehicles used as shared taxis
- **Tourism**: Varadero, Cayo Coco, Trinidad (UNESCO)
