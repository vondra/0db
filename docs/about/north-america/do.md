## Railway

### Defunct inter-city. Santo Domingo Metro active.

**Santo Domingo Metro Line 1** (2009) + **Line 2** (2013) — first metro system on a Caribbean island, ~70 stations combined. No inter-city passenger railway.

| Line | Length | Stations | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Line 1 (N-S) | 14.5 km | 16 | Centro de los Héroes–Villa Mella |
| Line 2 (E-W) | 20 km | 17 | Pantoja–Isabelita |

## Industrial

### GEM — 73 plants, operating, ~6,132 MW

Gas/LNG 18 + oil/HFO 16 + solar 14 + wind 8 + hydro 9 + coal 5 + bioenergy 3. **Largest Caribbean generation fleet**. **Quisqueya I+II gas 880 MW** — largest combined cycle. **Itabo I+II coal 437 MW**. **AES Andrés LNG 319 MW**.

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Gold/silver**: Pueblo Viejo (Barrick Gold/Newmont, Sánchez Ramirez) — largest gold mine in Caribbean
- **Nickel**: Falcondo (Glencore, Monseñor Nouel)
- **Tourism**: Punta Cana, Las Terrenas, Samaná — world's busiest all-inclusive resort zone
- **Sugar**: La Romana (Casa de Campo), San Pedro — Central Romana (Gulf+Western legacy)
- **Free-trade zones**: La Romana, San Isidro — garment, medical device exports
