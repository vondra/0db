## Road traffic

San Marino's entire road network falls within Italian (IT) H3 hexes at resolution 4. No standalone San Marino road layer is enriched — all road segments are captured and enriched under the Italy pipeline.

## Railway

### San Marino has no railway.

The historic **Ferrovia Rimini–San Marino** (1932–1944) was a 32 km narrow-gauge electric tramway connecting Rimini to San Marino city. It was bombed and destroyed in 1944 during WWII and never rebuilt. There are no current plans for reconstruction.

## Industrial

### GEM — 0 plants

No power plants in Global Energy Monitor. San Marino imports all electricity from Italy (ENEL) under a bilateral agreement managed by **AASS** (Azienda Autonoma di Stato per i Servizi).

## Notes

San Marino (61 km²) claims to be the world's oldest republic, founded in 301 AD by stonemason Marinus of Rab. It is an enclave entirely surrounded by Italy (Emilia-Romagna and Marche regions) with no coastline. The country has the world's highest vehicle density (~1,200 vehicles per 1,000 people) — traffic through the capital and Monte Titano is the primary noise source. San Marino implements EU-aligned noise regulation via the **Legge 30 luglio 2009 n.98** (environmental noise). Strategic noise maps are produced by the Segreteria di Stato per il Territorio. Monte Titano and the Three Towers (Guaita, Cesta, Montale) are a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Federico Fellini International Airport (RMI) in Rimini, ~20 km away, is the nearest airport.
