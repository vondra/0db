## Road traffic

### Class defaults only

IANAV (Instituto de Aeronáutica e Navegação Aérea) and AIAS publish no open road AADT. CNOSSOS class defaults applied with Praia Tier-1 boost (×2.0) and Tier-2 boosts (×1.4) for Mindelo (São Vicente) and Santa Maria (Sal). Ten islands; no fixed links between them.

**86k road segments enriched (100% coverage).** Island nation — complete coverage.

## Railway

### Cabo Verde has no railway.

No railway exists. All inter-island transport by ferry or TACV/inter-island air.

## Industrial

### GEM — 8 plants, ~46 MW

- **Cabeólica Wind** — 26 MW across 4 islands (Santiago, São Vicente, Sal, Boa Vista); commissioned 2012, one of Africa's first utility-scale wind farms
- **ELECTRA diesel thermal** — each island has one or more diesel generator sets (Wartsila, MAN); main supply for non-wind islands
- **Sal Solar** — expanding rooftop and ground-mount solar on tourist islands

Cabo Verde is a regional renewable energy leader: wind + solar covered ~60% of electricity in 2023.

## Notes

Cabo Verde (formerly Cape Verde) is an Atlantic archipelago ~570 km off Senegal. The country has no rivers — desalination plants supply all fresh water. Cesária Évora, the world-famous morna singer known as the "barefoot diva," was from Mindelo, São Vicente. The country has a large diaspora particularly in the Netherlands (Rotterdam), USA (New England), and Portugal. Sal (SID/GVAC) and Santiago/Praia (RAI/GVNP) airports handle international traffic; TACV Cabo Verde Airlines operates domestic and inter-island services. Portuguese is the official language; Kriolu (Cape Verdean Creole) is widely spoken.
