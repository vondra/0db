## Railway

Lietuvos Geležinkeliai (LTG Link), the Lithuanian national rail operator, does not publish GTFS publicly. National rail corridors (Vilnius-Kaunas-Klaipėda, Vilnius-Minsk) not enriched.

## Road traffic

Lietuvos automobilių kelių direkcija publishes only aggregate statistics. OSM defaults applied.

## Public transit

All available Lithuanian GTFS feeds (Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda, Panevėžys, Alytus, Šiauliai) are bus/trolleybus only — no tram or rail. Lithuania has no tram networks.

## Industrial

- GPPD power plants (NACE 35)

## Validation

Lithuania implements END via the Law on Environmental Protection. Strategic noise maps produced by Agentura (Environment Agency).
