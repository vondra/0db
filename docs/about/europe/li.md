## Road traffic

Liechtenstein's entire road network falls within Swiss (CH) and Austrian (AT) H3 hexes at resolution 4. No standalone Liechtenstein road layer is enriched — all road segments are captured and enriched under the Switzerland and Austria pipelines.

## Railway

### ÖBB Feldkirch–Buchs line

The single railway through Liechtenstein is the Austrian Federal Railways (ÖBB) **Feldkirch (AT) ↔ Schaan-Vaduz ↔ Buchs (CH)** line. Enriched as part of the Austria railway layer.

- **Schaan-Vaduz** is the only station in Liechtenstein
- ~30 passenger trains/day each direction (RegionalExpress + S-Bahn Vorarlberg)
- Liechtenstein operates no domestic railway of its own

No power plants in Global Energy Monitor. Liechtenstein imports electricity from Switzerland (EKW/Axpo) via the **Liechtensteinische Kraftwerke (LKW)** distribution network. A small run-of-river hydro station exists on the Rhine but is below GEM threshold.

## Notes

Liechtenstein (160 km²) is one of only two doubly-landlocked countries in the world (the other is Uzbekistan) — surrounded entirely by Switzerland and Austria, both of which are also landlocked. The **Rhine Valley floor** (Vaduz, Schaan, Balzers, Triesen) houses most of the population and all significant industry. Key employers include **Hilti** (power tools, HQ Schaan), **Ivoclar** (dental materials), **ThyssenKrupp Presta** (steering systems), and **LGT Bank** — Liechtenstein is a significant private banking centre (~30% of GDP from financial services). Liechtenstein abolished its military in 1868; a police force of ~80 officers maintains internal security. No airport exists — nearest options are St. Gallen–Altenrhein (ACH, 15 km) or Zurich ZRH (~70 km). Liechtenstein implements noise regulation via **Verordnung zum Schutz vor Lärm (LSV)**, aligned with Swiss standards.
