## Road traffic

### Road defaults

Niger publishes no open per-segment AADT, so roads fall back to the global class defaults scaled by Niger's traffic factor **≈ 1.201** (vehicles-per-km). Only the major classes (motorway, trunk, primary, and their on/off-ramps) are scaled; local roads and the vehicle mix use the global default — the engine applies no per-city tiers or country-specific splits.

| OSM class | Default AADT |
|---|---:|
| Motorway | 30,000 × 1.201 ≈ 36,030 |
| Trunk | 15,000 × 1.201 ≈ 18,015 |
| Primary | 9,000 × 1.201 ≈ 10,809 |
| Secondary / tertiary / residential | 3,000 / 800 / 500 (world default) |
| Service / track / unclassified | 250 / 5 / 1,340 (world default) |

## Railway

### Virtually NO operational railway

Niger has no functioning passenger or freight railway. The only rail infrastructure is the **SONICHAR industrial coal line** (~30 km, Anou Araren↔Tchirozérine) serving the coal power station near Agadez. The planned Niamey-Dosso line (142 km) was never completed.

## Industrial

### GEM — 8 plants, operating, ~262 MW

Solar 5 + oil/gas (diesel/HFO) 2 + coal/cement captive 1. **Gorou Banda 100 MW** (diesel) + **Niamey Issoufou 89 MW** (HFO) — Niamey thermal cluster. **Badaguichiri Cement 30 MW** (SNC captive). **Gorou Banda Solar 30 MW**. 4 small solar (Malbaza, Kéita, Bilma, Dogondoutchi).

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Uranium**: Arlit/Akokan — **SOMAIR** (Orano, still operating), **COMINAK** (closed 2021, ore exhausted). Niger was **world's #4 uranium producer**. France-Niger uranium relationship severed after 2023 coup.
- **Oil**: **Agadem** (CNPC) — Niger's first oil export pipeline to Benin opened 2024
- **Cement**: SNC Malbaza/Badaguichiri (Société Nigérienne de Cimenterie)
- **SONICHAR coal** (~36 MW, Tchirozérine — supplies Agadez/Arlit mining region)
- **~80% of territory is Sahara/Sahel desert** — population concentrated in narrow southern strip
- **Military junta since July 2023** — French troops expelled, Wagner/Africa Corps present
