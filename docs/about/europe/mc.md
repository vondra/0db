## Road traffic

Monaco's entire road network falls within French (FR) H3 hexes at resolution 4. No standalone Monaco road layer is enriched — all road segments are captured and enriched under the France pipeline.

## Railway

### SNCF through Monaco

The RFN (Réseau Ferré National) Nice–Ventimiglia mainline passes through Monaco underground (Monaco-Monte-Carlo station). Enriched as part of the France railway layer.

- **Monaco-Monte-Carlo** — underground station; Nice Ville–Ventimiglia service, ~45 trains/day each direction
- **TGV access** via Nice-Ville or Ventimiglia (Trenitalia)

## Notes

Monaco (2.08 km²) is the world's second-smallest sovereign state and the world's most densely populated country (~19,000/km²). It is entirely surrounded by France (Alpes-Maritimes département) and the Mediterranean Sea. The **Formula 1 Monaco Grand Prix** (Circuit de Monaco, 3.337 km street circuit) is the noisiest recurring event in the principality — peak race noise exceeds 130 dB(A) at trackside. Key landmarks: Casino de Monte-Carlo, Palais Princier, Port Hercule, Fairmont hairpin. Monaco implements noise regulation under the environmental code aligned with French norms; the Direction de l'Environnement monitors ambient levels.
