## Road traffic

### Road defaults

Burkina Faso publishes no open per-segment AADT, so roads fall back to the global class defaults scaled by Burkina Faso's traffic factor **≈ 1.299** (vehicles-per-km). Only the major classes (motorway, trunk, primary, and their on/off-ramps) are scaled; local roads and the vehicle mix use the global default — the engine applies no per-city tiers or country-specific splits.

| OSM class | Default AADT |
|---|---:|
| Motorway | 30,000 × 1.299 ≈ 38,970 |
| Trunk | 15,000 × 1.299 ≈ 19,485 |
| Primary | 9,000 × 1.299 ≈ 11,691 |
| Secondary / tertiary / residential | 3,000 / 800 / 500 (world default) |
| Service / track / unclassified | 250 / 5 / 1,340 (world default) |

## Railway

### Sitarail Abidjan↔Ouagadougou — ~520 km in BF, meter gauge (1,000 mm). Bolloré concession since 1995.

**West Africa's most important inland freight corridor.** Carries fuel, cement, manufactured goods from Abidjan port to landlocked BF. Overnight Ouaga↔Bobo passenger train.

**Kaya branch** (~100 km, barely operational). Tambao manganese line never completed.

| Context | pax/day | frt/day |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Sitarail main** (Ouaga↔Bobo↔CI border) | 2 | 4 |
| **Kaya branch** (barely operational) | 0 | 1 |

## Industrial

### GEM — 11 plants, operating, ~380 MW

Solar 8 + oil/gas (HFO/diesel) 3. **Essakane Gold Mine 57 MW** (IAMGOLD captive HFO). **Kossodo 54.9 MW** (diesel, Ouaga) + **Komsilga 54 MW** (HFO/diesel, Ouaga). **Zagtouli Solar 37+13 MW** (West Africa's largest at commissioning 2017). **Kodeni Solar 38 MW** + **Nagréongo 30 MW** + **Pâ Solar 30 MW** + **Zina 26.6 MW** + **Zano 24 MW**. **Essakane Solar 14.9 MW** (mine captive).

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Gold mining** — BF is **Africa's #4-5 gold producer**: Essakane (IAMGOLD, largest), Houndé (Endeavour), Inata, Bissa (Nordgold), Kiéré, Wahgnion (Lilium)
- **SOFITEX cotton** — one of West Africa's largest cotton producers
- **Tambao manganese** — large deposit, undeveloped (rail never completed)
- **Cement**: CIMFASO (Ouagadougou), Diamond Cement, CIM Metal Group
- **Security crisis** — jihadist insurgency expanding from Mali since 2015, ~2M internally displaced
