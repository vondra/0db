## Road traffic

### Road defaults

Fiji publishes no open per-segment AADT, so roads fall back to the global class defaults scaled by Fiji's traffic factor **≈ 1.082** (vehicles-per-km). Only the major classes (motorway, trunk, primary, and their on/off-ramps) are scaled; local roads and the vehicle mix use the global default — the engine applies no per-city tiers or country-specific splits.

| OSM class | Default AADT |
|---|---:|
| Motorway | 30,000 × 1.082 ≈ 32,460 |
| Trunk | 15,000 × 1.082 ≈ 16,230 |
| Primary | 9,000 × 1.082 ≈ 9,738 |
| Secondary / tertiary / residential | 3,000 / 800 / 500 (world default) |
| Service / track / unclassified | 250 / 5 / 1,340 (world default) |

## Railway

### Fiji has NO public railway.

Fiji Sugar Corporation operated narrow-gauge (610 mm) sugar cane tramways connecting cane fields to mills (Lautoka, Ba, Rarawai, Labasa). These are industrial lines for cane transport only — not public passenger or freight railways. Largely defunct/seasonal.

## Industrial

### GEM — 2 plants, operating, ~90 MW

Hydro 1 + wind 1. **Wailoa 80 MW** (hydropower, Monasavu Dam, Viti Levu highlands — Fiji's largest power plant). **Butoni Wind Farm 10 MW** (Viti Levu, 2007). Most of Fiji's remaining generation is diesel (Fiji Electricity Authority) below GEM threshold.

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Fiji Sugar Corporation** — 4 sugar mills (Lautoka, Ba, Rarawai, Labasa)
- **Vatukoula/Emperor Gold Mine** — one of Pacific's oldest (intermittent operations)
- **Fiji Water** (Yaqara) — globally recognized bottled water brand
- **PAFCO** (Levuka, Ovalau) — tuna processing, Pacific Fishing Company
- **Tourism** — #1 foreign exchange earner (Denarau, Mamanuca, Yasawa Islands, Coral Coast)
- **Cement**: Fiji Cement
- **Brewery**: South Pacific Distilleries (Fiji Gold, Fiji Bitter)
