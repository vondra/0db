## Railway

### Defunct. All railway traffic set to zero.

Barbados Government Railway closed 1937. Track long removed.

## Industrial

### GEM — 11 plants, operating, ~83 MW

Solar PV 6 + oil/diesel 3 + wind 2. **Trents Generating Station ~60 MW** oil — main Barbados Light & Power baseload. Rapid rooftop solar growth (~50 MW installed, mostly behind-meter).

### Key sites not NACE classified
- **Rum**: Mount Gay (world's oldest brand, est. 1703), Foursquare, St. Nicholas Abbey
- **Sugar**: Barbados birthplace of Caribbean rum/sugar industry; production now minimal
- **Tourism**: Platinum Coast (west), Bridgetown historic garrison (UNESCO)
- **Cricket**: Kensington Oval (Bridgetown) — Test match venue, Garfield Sobers birthplace
- **Republic since 2021**: removed British monarch; Dame Sandra Mason first President
