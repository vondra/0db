## Road traffic

### Class defaults only

The Direcção das Estradas publishes no open AADT. CNOSSOS class defaults applied with São Tomé city Tier-1 boost (×2.0). Two islands — São Tomé and Príncipe — with no fixed link; all inter-island transport by ferry or light aircraft.

## Railway

### São Tomé and Príncipe has no railway.

The historic cocoa plantation railways (roça railways) were narrow-gauge field tramways, all dismantled by the mid-20th century.

## Notes

São Tomé and Príncipe is Africa's second-smallest country (1,001 km²) and second-least populous. The islands straddle the equator in the Gulf of Guinea and are among the world's most biodiverse island ecosystems. Once the world's largest cocoa producer ("roça" plantation system), the islands still produce fine cacao — Claudio Corallo's artisanal chocolate is internationally recognized. Príncipe is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve with growing ecotourism. The country gained independence from Portugal in 1975. São Tomé International Airport (TMS) handles TAP Air Portugal, TAAG Angola Airlines, and Ethiopian Airlines connections. Offshore oil exploration in the Nigeria-São Tomé Joint Development Zone has so far found limited commercial quantities.
